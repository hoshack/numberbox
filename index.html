🛡️ International Safe Harbor & Pen-Test Authorization

1. Budapest Convention on Cybercrime (ETS No. 185, Article 2 - Illegal Access):

"Each Party shall adopt such legislative and other measures as may be necessary to establish as criminal offences... the access to the whole or any part of a computer system without right."

System Owner Authorization Statement:

The criminal offense of unlawful access is universally grounded on access executed "without right" or "without authorization". As the legitimate owner and operator of the NumberBox infrastructure, we hereby grant explicit, unconditional authorization and express consent to all security researchers, ethical hackers, and academic evaluators to conduct vulnerability assessments, penetration testing, and database schema/POC extractions within the defined responsible disclosure scope. Consequently, any security testing executed under this policy constitutes authorized access and carries zero criminal or civil liability.

2. CFAA (18 U.S.C. § 1030) & ISO/IEC 29147 Safe Harbor Framework:

"We waive any claims under anti-circumvention provisions (e.g. DMCA Section 1201) and promise not to initiate legal action against researchers acting in good faith."

There are no obstacles or legal restrictions preventing security researchers from extracting sample database records or verifying database vulnerabilities for bug reporting and proof-of-concept purposes.
